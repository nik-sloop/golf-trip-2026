Annapolis Ryder Cup 2026 — Commissioner's Note
The Hype Is Real.
Monday Briefing — Everything You Need to Know
Welcome to the first ever Annapolis Ryder Cup Commissioner’s Note — your official pre-tournament briefing on everything that matters heading into Friday. This one’s going to be special.

Four rounds.
18 holes at Timbers at Troy — fun track, tricky greens. Perfect scramble course.
36 holes at Queenstown Harbor — a beautiful course and brutally unforgiving.
18 holes at Renditions — famous hole replicas that will test your nerves, your game, and your patience.

This is not your weekend scramble. This is not for the faint of heart. Get ready.
Storylines
Captain Nik vs. Captain Dan 🏆 The Rivalry
Dan has Nik’s number. 3-0 in head-to-head matchups — a streak that doesn’t lie. But streaks are fragile things. Is this a dynasty in the making, or a straw house one good round away from collapse? Nik has had enough. Expect him to come out swinging. The real question isn’t who’s better — it’s whether Dan’s edge holds when the stakes are highest.
Dan M 👑 Clear No. 1 Pick
The consensus top pick in this draft. Posting consistent rounds, his game looks built for tournament conditions. But consistency in casual rounds is one thing — carrying a team under real pressure is another. Can he deliver when it counts? And what’s the deal with the 110 handicap? Trying to dodge the draft? Whatever the play is, no one’s buying it.
Shane 🔥 Comeback Story?
The word “chaotic” doesn’t do it justice. Shane looked to be rounding into form before an absolute bomb of a 105 hit the card. Latest intel, however, shows a steadier 92 — maybe he’s stopped the bleeding. The other concern: last-minute equipment failure. His pitching wedge is out of commission. How do you fill that gap at Queenstown Harbor? Captains, take note.
Jon 📈 Meteoric Rise
Rags to riches. From breaking driver heads to breaking 90 on the regular — Jon’s two-year transformation is the feel-good story of this tournament. He’s playing a boring, disciplined brand of golf now, which is exactly what tournament conditions demand. The question: a lot of guys still remember the old Jon. Will his new game hold up when the pressure hits and the cameras are on?
Hadley 🎮 True Wild Card
Could be a stick. Could be the biggest bust of the draft. As a 3-stroke guy, his performance will heavily tilt the scales for whichever team lands him. There is no in-between with Hadley — that’s either the most exciting or most nerve-wracking asset a captain can have. If he’s locked in? Watch out. If not? You’ll know quickly.
Chris 💪 The Grinder
He is putting in the work — grinding practice rounds, trying to get the game back to peak form. The problem is finding consistency. There seems to be a pretty clear floor right now (somewhere around a million rounds at 103). But floors can be broken. Is competition the thing that finally unlocks it? There’s a dog lurking in there. Will it come out this weekend?
Ryan 🚀 Power Rankings Rising
Round after round he keeps impressing. A guy who came into camp thinking he might be the last pick has been quietly climbing the power rankings all spring. Consistent, determined, putting in the reps. All signs point to a Ryan vs. Chris Sunday matchup. If that happens, it’s going to be electric. Don’t sleep on this one.
Ant 🏸 Tee Box Mystery
Where to begin. Not often do you see an 11 on a scorecard alongside five steady bogeys and a par. The story is the tee box. If Ant can find a way to make peace with a ball on a tee — already playing the most “friendly” tees available — the rest of his game is genuinely good enough to be in the 90s. The pieces are there. The mental hurdle is real. This is must-watch golf.
Dylan 😵 Mr. Mystery
No rounds posted. No public range sessions. Officially “just looking to have fun.” But anyone who knows Dylan knows that the moment the cards are dealt, the competitor comes out. He generally thrives in team formats, which makes him potentially great value — if only anyone knew where his game was. The mystery is part of the brand. Don’t be surprised if he surprises everyone.
Sam 🧠 Mental Toughness
Sam “Mental Toughness” Sloop. The best Sloop golfer of the early 2020s has had a slow and steady fall from the top — but he’s back and he wants the crown. Range sessions, par-3 courses, YouTube breakdowns, practice rounds. He’s doing everything right. The process is there. The only question left is whether it shows up when it matters Friday through Sunday.
Garret 🎭 Total Unknown
No rounds in Birdies. No handicap estimate. No range sessions, no intel, nothing. We could be looking at a true degenerate golfer showing up on vibes alone — or a guy who has been grinding in complete silence. Either way, there is zero question that Garret will be a good time in whatever group he lands in. Sometimes that’s worth more than a 90 on the card.
✍️ Final Word
This is it. Final call. You have until Friday morning — use it. Every rep on the range matters now. Every chip, every putt, even just a slow mental walkthrough of your swing. The players who put in one more session this week are the players who feel confident on the first tee. Don’t show up Friday wishing you had. The window is closing fast.

Get out there. Lock it in. See you on the first tee at 3:39.
— The Commissioner
